You don't prove you're done by reading code. You prove it by **looking at the real thing**.

## Let the tutor check

In your `my-corner` session, send:

<PromptCard>
/academy:check 0
</PromptCard>

The tutor asks for your page's address, opens it, compares it with the file in your folder, and tells you what passed. It also saves a short report in your folder.
