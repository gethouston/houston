The full program takes about **100 hours**, part-time. Each course ships a project. From Course 2 on, you'll build **your own product**, one slice at a time, and launch it at the end.

:::note[Pilot]
This is an early pilot. Course 0 is complete. Courses 1 and 2 have their first lessons. The rest is on the way.
:::

## Level 1 · Foundations

| Course | You'll ship | Status |
|---|---|---|
| **0 · Hello, internet** (1 h) | A personal page, live at a URL you open on your phone | ✅ Available |
| **1 · How software works** (10 h) | A page that shows live data from an API, with a secret key kept safe | 🟡 2 lessons |
| **2 · Think like a builder** (8 h) | Your product's brief, its slices, and slice 1 as a prototype | 🟡 1 lesson |
| **3 · Your workshop** (6 h) | Your product on GitHub, with save points, a proposal and a rollback | Coming |

## Level 2 · Going live

| Course | You'll ship |
|---|---|
| **4 · Going live** | Your product at a URL, with a preview for every change |
| **5 · Data that survives** | A real database, and a backup you've restored |
| **6 · Users and permissions** | Login and roles, where nobody sees anyone else's data |
| **7 · Secrets and not getting hacked** | A security review of your product, with everything fixed |

## Level 3 · A real product

| Course | You'll ship |
|---|---|
| **8 · Connecting to the world** | Email, an AI feature, and (optional) payments |
| **9 · Keeping it alive** | Error alerts, uptime checks, cost alerts, and an incident drill |
| **10 · Launch and learn** | Your first 10 real users, and one slice built from their feedback |

## Level 4 · Lead

| Course | You'll ship |
|---|---|
| **11 · Leading agents** | A big feature built from a brief alone, accepted on evidence |
| **Electives** | AI agents with Houston, payments, automations, mobile, internal tools |
| **Capstone** | Your product, live with real users for two weeks |
