## 2. The premise (0:40–2:00)
- Writing code used to be the gate. Now agents write it.
- So why isn't everyone building software? Because the hard part moved.
- It's **the world around the code**: what's a terminal, what's localhost, what's an API key and where does it go, how do I put this on the internet, how do I keep my users' data safe.
- Show 3 real headlines (on screen, with sources): the Tea app leak, the Lovable / Supabase row-level-security leaks, "my page is blank after deploying".
- "These aren't coding problems. They're *understanding* problems."

## 3. The second problem: GTA 6 (2:00–3:30)
- "The first thing most people type into an agent is their whole dream." On screen: *"Build me GTA 6, but multiplayer."*
- Why it fails, for agents and for great teams: you can't test it, you can't tell what broke.
- The fix: one slice at a time. Show the slice diagram from Course 2.

## 4. What Houston Academy is (3:30–5:00)
