Where these files came from
---------------------------
Downloaded verbatim from the Fontshare CDN, via the same stylesheet the
website's `src/_includes/base.njk` loads at runtime:

  https://api.fontshare.com/v2/css?f[]=general-sans@400,500,600,700

The `.ttf` sources that stylesheet lists, one per weight:

  400  https://cdn.fontshare.com/wf/MFQT7HFGCR2L5ULQTW6YXYZXXHMPKLJ3/YWQ244D6TACUX5JBKATPOW5I5MGJ3G73/7YY3ZAAE3TRV2LANYOLXNHTPHLXVWTKH.ttf
  500  https://cdn.fontshare.com/wf/3RZHWSNONLLWJK3RLPEKUZOMM56GO4LJ/BPDRY7AHVI3MCDXXVXTQQ76H3UXA63S3/SB2OEB6IKZPRR6JT4GFJ2TFT6HBB6AZN.ttf
  600  https://cdn.fontshare.com/wf/K46YRH762FH3QJ25IQM3VAXAKCHEXXW4/ISLWQPUZHZF33LRIOTBMFOJL57GBGQ4B/3ZLMEXZEQPLTEPMHTQDAUXP5ZZXCZAEN.ttf
  700  https://cdn.fontshare.com/wf/KWXO5X3YW4X7OLUMPO4X24HQJGJU7E2Q/VOWUQZS3YLP66ZHPTXAFSH6YACY4WJHT/NIQ54PVBBIWVK3PFSOIOUJSXIJ5WTNDP.ttf

They ship as `.ttf` rather than the `.woff2` the browser gets because satori
reads TTF/OTF/WOFF only. The site loads 500/600/700 (the wordmark's weight and
its two heavier siblings); the certificate additionally needs 400, which is the
same family cut from the same release.
