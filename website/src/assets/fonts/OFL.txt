A variable font (weight axis 100–900), latin subset, retrieved from Google Fonts
(fonts.gstatic.com). Full OFL text:
https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/

The license permits self-hosting and redistribution with the software. We ship the
latin subset only (the marketing landing page is English); the es/pt subpages do not
use this face. The landing declares the 300–800 range of the axis and drives the whole
page from this single humanist grotesque — a light 300 display, 400 body, 600 UI —
approximating Mercury's airy low-contrast display voice for the partnership prototype.
