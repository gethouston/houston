# App Review notes

Houston lets people create AI Employees, give them missions, exchange messages, and review work with their team. The native apps connect to the same Houston account as the web app.

Sign in with Apple is available on iOS. Reviewers can also choose email sign-in, enter an address they control, and enter the one-time code delivered to that inbox. No paid account is required to inspect the basic app.

Account deletion is in More > Settings > Delete account. The confirmation explains the effect and asks the person to type the confirmation word.

Purchases and plan changes happen on the web. The mobile app offers access to existing accounts and does not initiate purchases or show purchase links.

Push notifications tell a person when an AI Employee has an update or a mission needs attention. Notification permission is requested in context and can be declined.
